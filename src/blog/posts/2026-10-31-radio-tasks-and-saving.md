A short one this time, but two of these changes made my festival management game feel a lot more like a real game to me. One gives you something to do during the concert. The other means you don't lose everything when you close it.

## Radio tasks during the concert

I keep coming back to the same problem. Once the gates were open and the band was playing, there were long quiet stretches where I just sat and watched. Fights and fainting happen, but not often enough. So the radio now hands you small jobs while the show is on.

They show up as yellow cards at the top of the radio panel, at most two at a time. Each one has a countdown, a cash bonus and usually a one-click button. There are four for now:

- **Crush at the front of the stage.** Send two security guards to open up the crowd before someone gets hurt.
- **Long queue.** Get the line at a bar or food stand back under 8 people.
- **Crowd in the dark.** People are gathering somewhere with no light. Send security, and maybe put a lighting pole there next time.
- **Lost bag.** Someone dropped a bag. A glowing bag is somewhere in the crowd, and you have to find it and click on it.

The lost bag one is my favourite. It's a little like a hidden object game in the middle of a concert. In one test I took too long, and the radio just said the bag wasn't found and the owner was complaining. Fair enough.

Finish a task and you get the bonus and a bit of reputation. Miss one and it costs you: somebody faints at the front, people in the queue get grumpy, a fight starts in the dark, or you lose a reputation point. Bigger crowds get more tasks (one more for every 50 people, up to 10 a night), metal nights get a few more than the others, and if nothing has happened for a while the radio finds you something.

## Saving your game

This one is a bit embarrassing. Until now there was no saving at all. Quitting the game showed a warning that your progress would be lost, and it really was. Every test started from an empty field.

Now the game saves on its own at the start of every day and after every concert, and you have three save slots of your own. The main menu has a Continue button that loads your latest save. You can't save while a concert is running, which felt right: you can't pause a real show either. It stores everything I could think of, from half-finished buildings and cables to your staff, your bank loans, your calendar and the worn grass.

## Rotating buildings freely

Buildings no longer have to face one of four directions. R turns them 90 degrees, Shift+R turns them back, and if you hold the middle mouse button while placing and drag sideways you can turn them to any angle. New buildings also snap neatly next to the ones already there, and holding Alt turns that off. Crooked rows of food stalls are now possible, and I have already built some.

## Feet that don't slide

Last post I was proud of the new 3D visitors. Then I zoomed in and noticed they were gliding, like they were on ice. Their walk animation moved them forward and then snapped them back every step. I fixed it so their feet now move with the ground, they turn smoothly instead of spinning on the spot, and people walking past give way to people standing still instead of shoving them.

## A fix for fights

I sped up concert time a while ago, so one hour of game time is now six real minutes instead of ten. What I didn't notice is that this also shrank the time you get to break up a fight to 48 seconds. One security guard simply couldn't get there in time. Now fights always give you 80 real seconds and fainting gives you 150, at normal speed, no matter how fast the game clock runs.

## What's next

Water and toilets that need pipes are still on the list. For the radio I'd like a photographer who asks you to catch a special moment, encores, a special guest and requests from the band.
